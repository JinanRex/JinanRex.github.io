A LITTLE BACKGROUND

Hi, I'm Hongyi.

I'm at Zhejiang University, in the CADIDG group of the CAD Lab, advised by Prof. Yingcai Wu.

My research interests lie in natural language processing and large language models, with a focus on misinformation generation, misinformation detection, and fact verification.

The question that keeps me curious: how do we tell whether a model is getting better — or just getting better at fooling the test?

AtomEval: Validity-Aware Atomic Evaluation of Adversarial Claim Rewriting in Fact Verification

Hongyi Cen, Mingxin Wang, Yule Liu, Jingyi Zheng, Hanze Jia, Tan Tang

Did it fool the verifier,
or did it change the claim?

AtomEval checks whether adversarial rewrites preserve the original false proposition, separating valid verifier evasion from proposition-changing rewrites.
